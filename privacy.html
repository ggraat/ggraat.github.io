Privacy Policy – Nixplay sync

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Nixplay sync is a personal tool used only by its owner (GitHub user ggraat), in his own Google account.

Data accessed

  • Photos that the user explicitly selects in the Google Photos Picker. No other Google Photos data is accessed.
  • The user's email address, used only to send the user notification emails.

How data is used

Selected photos are downloaded in reduced size and sent by email to the user's own Nixplay photo frame address. Nothing else is done with them.

Storage and sharing

The app does not store photos or personal data on any server of its own and does not share data with third parties, other than delivering the selected photos to the user's own Nixplay account by email, at the user's request. Picker sessions are deleted after use.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
