Nixplay sync

Nixplay sync is a small personal Google Apps Script made by ggraat. It runs in the owner's own Google account and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  1. Every few weeks it emails the owner a link to the Google Photos Picker.
  2. The owner selects photos in the Picker.
  3. The script downloads only those selected photos in a reduced size and emails them to the owner's own Nixplay digital photo frame.

Google data used

The app requests read-only access to photos the user explicitly selects via the Google Photos Picker API (photospicker.mediaitems.readonly), plus permission to send email and run scheduled triggers. It cannot see any other photos in the library.
